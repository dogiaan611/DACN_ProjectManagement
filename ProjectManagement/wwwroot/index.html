Discover the features that help you manage your projects

Powerful agile boards

  • Scrum boards

    Scrum boards help agile teams break large, complex projects into manageable pieces of work so focused teams ship faster.

  • Kanban boards

    Your teams can use flexible kanban boards to visualize workflows, limit work-in-progress, and maximize efficiency as a team.

  • Choose your own adventure

    FlowTeam is flexible enough whether it is Scrum, Kanban, or something in between.

Master every details

  • File attachments

    Keep everything organized. Upload images, documents, and design files directly to the task so your team has instant access to what they need.

  • Discussion & Collaboration

    Collaborate in context. Use comments and @mentions to provide feedback, ask questions, and keep everyone aligned without leaving the task.

  • Subtasks & Activity Log

    Break complex work into manageable subtasks. Track the full history of changes in the Activity tab to monitor progress and accountability.

Master your Sprints

  • Agile Sprint Planning

    Manage active sprints with ease. Set distinct timeframes, track story points, and monitor sprint status to keep your team focused on immediate goals.

  • Product Backlog

    Maintain a healthy pipeline of work. Organize pending tasks in the backlog and simply drag-and-drop them into active sprints when you're ready to execute.

  • Prioritization & Assignment

    Ensure the most important work gets done first. Assign tasks to team members and set priority levels (High, Medium, Low) directly from the board.
